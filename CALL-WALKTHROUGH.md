# Direct-assumption demo walkthrough

Open http://localhost:8506 and choose **Scenario lab**.

1. Expand **Edit assumptions**. Show the actual monthly spend for each channel, cost per 1,000 impressions, and three conversion rates. There are no hidden seasonal or growth multipliers.
2. Change Paid search monthly spend from $120,000 to $240,000. At the default rates, its monthly funded accounts increase from 490 to 980. Follow the channel funnel table below the inputs.
3. Change the opportunity-to-funded rate to test sales execution without increasing media spend.
4. Show the 1.65% annual management fee, initial contribution, recurring contribution and lifetime assumptions. Deposits become managed assets; the management fee generates company revenue.
5. Show **Customer economics**. Monthly ARPA is lifetime-average fee revenue per account per month. Lifetime fee revenue = monthly ARPA × 12 × lifetime years. Revenue LTV discounts future management fees. Acquisition CAC includes channel spend only; service, sales and overhead costs are excluded.
6. Reduce a channel's lifetime from 10 years to 2. Its modeled lifetime value falls, and its future accounts and assets run off sooner. Same-month funding is a simplifying assumption; each cohort is assumed to stay for exactly the selected planning lifetime.
7. Open **Forecast & variance** for the horizontal financial schedule. Scenario edits affect July 2026 onward only. Six simulated actual months remain unchanged.

All operating data is illustrative. The opening portfolio is a hypothetical $750M and 30,000 accounts; it is not an estimate of Embark's actual business. The 1.65% fee is the user-selected planning assumption, before separate rebates or tax adjustments. No claim is made that it equals net corporate revenue realization.

Opening accounts keep their illustrative 10-year lifetime and are distributed evenly across remaining months. Edited lifetimes apply to new forecast cohorts. Partial asset withdrawals are modeled separately from final account closure. No additional account attrition is layered on top.

The scenario comparison is against the reset example assumptions, not an undisclosed user budget. Data downloads include all entered channel and financial assumptions. Downloads are CSV/JSON, not formula-based Excel workbooks.
